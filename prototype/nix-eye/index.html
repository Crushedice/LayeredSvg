How it's put together

  • Stack, bottom to top: base (iris painted out) → iris and pupil inside the sclera clip → blink frame → glow. The lid frames have the eye opening cut out, so the live iris keeps moving during a blink and while Reticent holds the half-closed lid.
  • Face markings are a copy of the painted lines laid over the base and recoloured with hue-rotate per affect. Each blink frame has its own copy, because the slashes above the eye sit on the lid and move with it.
  • The pupil is split from the iris and the slit underneath is inpainted, so scaleX narrows or widens just the slit.
  • Gaze is in percent of the iris box. Saccades jump in 45 ms and hold for the affect's interval, with small microsaccades in between.
  • Every layer is generated by tools/build_eye_layers.py from the source PNGs.
